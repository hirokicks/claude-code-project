Somewhere between the tenth browser tab and the afternoon standup, there is a small window of genuine time. Not scheduled time. Not productive time. Just — yours.

The evidence is embarrassingly clear: the people who actually stop for lunch come back sharper, calmer, and less likely to send emails they'll regret at 4pm. It is not a controversial finding. It is just one that is very easy to ignore when the notifications don't pause.

Meridian was built for exactly this window. Not as an alternative to alcohol — alcohol was never really the point of a lunch ritual — but as a reason to pause properly. Something worth opening. Something that signals to everyone at the table: this hour is intentional.

The best conversations of your career probably happened over a long lunch. This is a hypothesis worth testing.

"The most productive thing you can do at noon is actually stop."
